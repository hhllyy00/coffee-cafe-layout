Our Story

A Local Neighbourhood Gem

Coffee Cafe is a locally owned coffee bar nestled in a Palmerston North neighbourhood. We pride ourselves on serving quality coffee in a comfortable, welcoming space.

Whether you're catching up with friends, studying for exams, or just need a quiet moment — our doors are always open.

  • Free Wi-Fi for students & workers

Design Notes — PixelMeister (Han Linyuan)

Color Scheme

Warm, coffee-inspired palette that evokes comfort and quality.

#3E2723
#D4A574
#F5E6D3
#8D6E63
#FAF6F0
  • Primary: Deep Coffee #3E2723 — headers, footer, text
  • Accent: Caramel #D4A574 — buttons, highlights, prices
  • Secondary: Warm Cream #F5E6D3 — about bg, cards
  • Background: Cream White #FAF6F0 — page base
  • Muted: #8D6E63 — secondary text

Font Scheme

  • Headings: Playfair Display (serif) — elegant, warm, artisanal cafe feel. Weights: 400/600/700/900
  • Body: Lato (sans-serif) — clean, modern, highly readable. Weights: 300/400/700/900
  • Fallback: Georgia / Times New Roman for serif; Helvetica Neue / Arial for sans
  • Sizes: H1 56px, H2 40px, H3 22-24px, body 14-16px

Page Layout Type

Jello Design — centered content with max-width 1100px.

  • Content stays centered regardless of screen size
  • Consistent reading experience across devices
  • Responsive breakpoints at 768px (stacks to single column)
  • Padding 24px on mobile for comfortable margins

Accessibility

  • High contrast text (dark on light / light on dark)
  • Semantic HTML5 tags (header, nav, section, footer)
  • Descriptive link text (no "click here")
  • Keyboard-navigable navigation
  • Responsive font sizes for readability
  • Alt text placeholders for all images
  • Consistent navigation placement on all pages

Page Sections

  • Header — logo + sticky navigation (Home/Menu/About/Visit Us/Contact)
  • Hero — headline, tagline, CTA button, warm gradient bg
  • Featured Menu — 3-column card grid with images, names, descriptions, prices
  • About — 2-column: image + story text + feature list
  • Visit Us — 2 cards: location/contact + opening hours table
  • Footer — brand, quick links, contact, social, copyright

Target Audience Fit

  • Warm brown/cream palette = cozy, welcoming for 16-40 age group
  • Clear menu & hours = students & workers need quick info
  • Free Wi-Fi mentioned = study-friendly signal
  • Affordable price points shown = matches budget-conscious audience
  • Social links = connects with younger demographic
